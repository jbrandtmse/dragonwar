## Intent

**Problem:** The machine has a complete game — Start, Hot seat, the ball lifecycle, devices and shots — and no way to read it. `src/presentation/backglass/` holds only a `.gitkeep` reading "Filled by Story 2.6", and the backbox quad that AC 1 maps a dot-matrix canvas onto has never existed: `cabinet_root` is authored childless and no backglass or backbox geometry is anywhere in the project.

**Approach:** Author a `vis_backbox` quad in the Blender source under `cabinet_root` and re-export; then build the DMD as three pure, headlessly-assertable modules (glyph font, frame composition, dot rasteriser) behind one thin Babylon `RawTexture` blit, driven from `FrameOutput` — the snapshot for continuous display, payload-complete events for end-of-ball — and wire it into the host's frame path.

## Boundaries & Constraints
