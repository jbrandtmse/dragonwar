## Intent

**Problem:**
- `cabinet_root` holds one mesh, the 400 x 100 mm DMD quad `vis_backbox`. The playfield floats in empty space: there is no cabinet body, no side rails, no lockdown bar, no legs, no backbox round the Backglass and no glass over the playfield.
- `gi_backbox`, `gi_cabinet` and `gi_arch` exist only as empty keys of `TABLE.giChannels`. Nothing in the scene is lit by them, and nothing in presentation can take a level for them.

**Approach:**
- `tools/make-placeholder-blend.py` gains a cabinet pass. Under `cabinet_root` it models a cabinet body, side rails, a lockdown bar, legs, a glass sheet and a backbox at the standard-body reference dimensions, fitted round this table, plus three lamp-position Empties named after the three GI channels. Everything is `vis_` only.
- Presentation gives the glass and the backbox translite their transparency in code, and creates one scoped light per GI channel at load. A `(channel, level)` seam drives the lights by intensity. Two dev hatches let the lead check the result in Chrome.
- `GiCommand`-driven behaviour stays Story 4.1's. The Walk-up stays Story 4.6's.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Frames used below.** The **level frame** is the table frame of AD-10 as `cabinet_root` sees it: millimetres, X right, Y away from the player, Z up, origin at the playfield's bottom-left corner, with `playfield_root` unpitched. `pivot_pitch` is at (257.2, 0, 0). A table point (y, z) pitched by θ lands at level depth `D = y·cos θ − z·sin θ` and height `H = z·cos θ + y·sin θ`.

- **Assembled height.** The top of `vis_head` is 1790 ± 2 mm above the feet of `vis_legs`. This is the planned value; it is not the research's "about 1930" (Design Notes, amendment candidate 2).
- **Nodes.** Exactly these eleven meshes are added, all with identity transforms:
  - direct children of `cabinet_root` (the five **objects**): `vis_cabinet`, `vis_lockbar`, `vis_legs`, `vis_top_glass`, `vis_head`;
  - children of `vis_cabinet`: `vis_cabinet_rail_l`, `vis_cabinet_rail_r`, `vis_cabinet_arch`;
  - children of `vis_head`: `vis_head_panel`, `vis_head_translite`, `vis_head_lightbox`.
  Plus exactly three mesh-less Empties, direct children of `cabinet_root`, named exactly as the keys of `TABLE.giChannels`.
- **Mesh contract (AD-11, AD-12).** Each new mesh has one material, one primitive, `uv_base` plus `uv_lightmap` (`TEXCOORD_1`), and `lightgroup: 'lg_cabinet'`. The Empties carry no property.
- **Materials.** Seven new `mat_art_<kind>` materials: colour and roughness only, metallic 0, no texture, base colour not pure white and roughness not 1.0 (so the exporter writes both factors).

  | `mat_art_cabinet_trim` | both rails, `vis_lockbar`, `vis_legs` | 0.20, 0.20, 0.22 | 0.35 |
  | `mat_art_cabinet_arch` | `vis_cabinet_arch` | 0.10, 0.12, 0.06 | 0.60 |

  | `mat_art_lightbox` | `vis_head_lightbox` only | 0.20, 0.14, 0.06 | 0.80 |

  Lit surfaces are dark on purpose: the scene has no exposure or tonemapping, so a pale surface sits at the LDR ceiling under the hemispheric light alone and a lamp cannot brighten it.
- **Budget.** Each of the five objects, counted with its descendants, has at most 2,000 triangles, in one level of detail. `pnpm check:size` stays within its 2.75 MB budget.
- **Playfield fit at every allowed pitch.** At `TUNING.pitchMinDeg` (6.0), `TABLE.reference.pitchDeg` (6.5) and `TUNING.pitchMaxDeg` (8.5), every vertex of every mesh under `playfield_root`:
  - is at least 5 mm below the plane of the glass's lower face;
  - has x strictly between the side walls' inner faces (−13.8 and 528.2);
  - has depth D below the arch panel's front face (1075);
  - has depth D above the front wall's inner face (−10), except `vis_plunger` and its descendants, whose rod passes through the front wall by design.
- **Backglass stays clear.** All eight corners of `vis_backbox` lie at least 10 mm above the plane of the glass's upper face, inside the speaker panel's outline in X and Z, and in front of the speaker panel's front face.
- **Transparency is set in presentation.** The glb carries the glass and the translite as opaque materials. `resolveCabinet()` sets `alpha` and `transparencyMode = PBRMaterial.PBRMATERIAL_ALPHABLEND` on exactly those two materials: `TOP_GLASS_ALPHA = 0.08`, `TRANSLITE_ALPHA = 0.55`.
- **Architectural lamps.**
  - One `PointLight` per key of `TABLE.giChannels`, named `` `light_${channel}` ``, created once per scene, parented to the Empty of the same name at local position zero. Three lights in all.
  - Each light is scoped with `includedOnlyMeshes` to its authored targets and to nothing else:
    - `gi_backbox` lights `vis_head_lightbox` (seen through the translite);
    - `gi_cabinet` lights `vis_cabinet`, `vis_cabinet_rail_l`, `vis_cabinet_rail_r` and `vis_lockbar`;
    - `gi_arch` lights `vis_cabinet_arch`.
  - `falloffType = PointLight.FALLOFF_STANDARD` with an authored `range`; diffuse (1.0, 0.85, 0.6) on all three. Authored intensity and range: `gi_backbox` 20 and 2.0 m; `gi_cabinet` 20 and 3.0 m; `gi_arch` 10 and 2.0 m. These sit inside the band the lead measured (intensity 1 to 20 on a dark PBR surface under standard falloff).
  - A channel's light intensity is `level × authored intensity`. The level is in [0, 1]. The default level is 1 for all three, because nothing in this tree emits a `GiCommand`.
  - Lights always stay enabled. A level change writes `intensity` only. No light is created, disposed, enabled or disabled after the first resolve.
  - The authoring table is keyed by `GiChannel` with unquoted keys. Channel names never appear as string literals under `src/`.
- **Fail fast.** A missing cabinet mesh, a cabinet mesh outside `cabinet_root`, a missing lamp Empty, or a glass or translite with no material throws from the first render frame, naming the node, so boot shows the error panel (AD-17).
- **Dev hatches** (same dev-only, console-only terms as the existing hatches):

  - `reset()` does not restore either: they are view state of the scene, not of the game loop.

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Expected Output / Behavior | Error Handling |
|----------|--------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| Boot with the shipped glb | first render frame | cabinet resolved; glass and translite alpha-blended; three lights exist at level 1 | No error expected |
| Cabinet mesh missing | glb with `vis_top_glass` renamed in its JSON chunk | the first-frame promise rejects | throws naming `vis_top_glass`; boot's error panel |
| Lamp Empty missing | glb with the `gi_arch` node renamed | the first-frame promise rejects | throws naming the channel's node |
| Cabinet mesh outside `cabinet_root` | scene where `vis_lockbar` is re-parented to `playfield_root` | `resolveCabinet()` rejects it | throws naming the node and `cabinet_root` |
| Resolve called every frame | second and later calls on one scene | no new light, no new material, same objects returned | No error expected |
| Scene rebuilt (WebGPU to WebGL2 fallback) | a second scene | its own three lights at the default level | No error expected |
| Level set | `setArchitecturalLevel(scene, channel, 0.25)` | that light's intensity is 0.25 × authored; the other two unchanged; `scene.lights.length` unchanged | No error expected |
| Level out of range or not finite | `−0.1`, `1.5`, `NaN`, `Infinity` | level unchanged | the setter throws a `RangeError` naming channel and value; the hatch logs a console error instead and ignores the call |
| Unknown channel at the hatch | `setArchitecturalLevel('gi_nope', 1)` | nothing changes | console error naming the value; ignored |
| Hatch level `null` | `setArchitecturalLevel(channel, null)` | the default level is restored | No error expected |
| Hide a mesh | `setNodeVisible(scene, name, false)` | that mesh's `isVisible` is false; its children stay visible | No error expected |
| Hide an unknown or mesh-less node | a missing name, or a lamp Empty's name | nothing changes | throws naming the node |
| Hatch before a scene exists | either hatch, no frame rendered yet | nothing changes | throws `no scene has rendered yet`, like `nodeScreenRect` |
| Pitch at 6.0 and at 8.5 | `applyPitch()` at the two bounds | no playfield vertex within 5 mm of the glass or outside the tub; no opaque cabinet mesh hides a flipper, an insert or the Backglass | No error expected |
