## Intent

**Problem:** Ten ledger entries survived every Epic 2 story's own adjudication gate -- each already judged real, owned, and not closable on existing evidence. They span three areas with different gates: the geometry test tree, the tunables file with its hashed provenance headers, and the ball controller. Until they are closed or consciously retired the ledger's open count means nothing, and four of the ten are specifically "the check does not catch its own subject" -- non-binding gates in an epic that recorded 70 vacuities, every one found by deliberate falsification and none by a passing run.

**Approach:** Take each entry to one of two terminal states -- resolved with cited evidence (a test, a commit, a measurement) or terminal with a stated reason and, where the reason is "not worth it", an observable `reopen_if=` probe. Re-measure every entry's premise at this tree before acting on it: three of the ten are already substantially closed by later stories that never reported back, and one had its premise corrected at the burn-down gate. Every check this story adds or repairs carries a named, applied, observed-red, reverted mutation (Rule 19). Golden changes are header-only.
