## Goal

Epic 2 turns Epic 1's placeholder vertical slice into a machine a stranger can play with no instructions. The real playfield geometry is drawn for the first time — the drain triangle, both Loops, the Ramp, the off-centre Dragon with the Lock lane between its legs, the DRAGON drop bank, Top lanes, slings and pops — and every device on it is wired into the table registry with a switch that registers reliably at the fastest speed the physics core can produce. On that geometry sits the whole standard game: Start and Hot seat for one to four players, the ball lifecycle, plunge and Skill shot, lane change, ball save, end-of-ball bonus and multiplier, tilt warnings, Tilt and Slam tilt, ball search, Match, game over and a return to a minimal Attract — all readable from a DMD Backglass with the inserts lit in the table's fixed colour grammar. Geometry was the first story and iterated with the rules that land on it; **the geometry, physics, device-vocabulary, lifecycle, display, lamp, ball-save and bonus halves are all now delivered**, and what remains is the last band of rules stories — tilt, ball search, Match and game over — plus one chartered correction to the skill shot's lit lane.

- **Tilt keeps the score and forfeits the bonus (Story 2.11, the next story).** FR-14/FR-15/FR-16 and the epic's five acceptance criteria: a `s_tilt_bob` closure with no closure inside `tiltWarningSpacingMs` increments the **current player's** `tiltWarnings` and fires `tilt_warning { player, remaining }`, with a closure inside the spacing window ignored; the bob is **never reset by command** — its physical decay plus `tiltSettleMs` is the settle. When the warning count equals the adjustment (**default 1**) the next closure fires `tilt { player }`, sets `machine.tilt`, disables flippers, slings, pops and autolaunch **together** via `CoilCommand disable`, **disarms ball save**, shows TILT on the Backglass, and the ball ends when the last ball in play drains. Warnings are per player. `s_slam_tilt` during any phase with a game running fires `slam_tilt`, ends **every** player's game without bonus, disables hardware and returns the machine to Attract. **Two normative details live outside `epics.md`:** the brief addendum is what makes *autolaunch* part of the disable set, and the PRD's own review logged that FR-15 says nothing about locked balls or the Mouth during a multiball Tilt — silence, not permission.
- **Ball search never releases locked balls while a mode timer runs** (Story 2.12), escalates through each device's declared `ballSearchOrder`, cancels and restarts on any switch closure, and only at its final stage issues the single `RecoverCommand` — after which `ballsInPlay` is corrected **from slot switches**. - **Match draws a multiple of ten from the seeded rules PRNG** at the configured probability (default 8%) and is display-only under free play (Story 2.13). Game over shows final scores by player; Attract returns after `attractMs` or on Start, with hardware disabled, `modes[]` empty and `players[]` cleared on the next Start.

## Technical Decisions

- **The tilt-bob and slam switches reach rules today and are SILENTLY DROPPED — this is Story 2.11's first real design decision, not a detail.** Physics already emits both: `createCabinetMechanics()` level-tracks the plumb bob's `isOverThreshold` and the slam detector's windowed nudge count, pushing `s_tilt_bob` / `s_slam_tilt` edges into the same `switchEvents` array `sim/loop` hands to `rules.step()`. But the devices layer's `buildPlayfieldSwitches()` **excludes the `tilt_bob` and `slam` settle classes by construction**, and no branch in its switch dispatch matches them — so they emit no device event of any kind, and AD-19 forbids anything outside `src/sim/rules/devices/` from consuming a raw `SwitchEvent`. **AD-19's event enumeration became BINDING at its 2026-09-06 amendment** ("a shipped event missing from it is a stale seam contract, not a documentation nicety"), so adding a tilt-bob/slam device event extends a spine-level enumeration and owes an AD-19 amendment in the same change. Budget that at planning.
- **The switch names may never be typed as literals, and the sanctioned derivation already exists.** `pnpm lint:boundaries` errors on an `s_`-prefixed literal outside `sim/table/dragonwar.ts`. Physics resolves both structurally from their **unique `SettleClass`** — exactly one `TABLE.switches` entry has `settleClass: 'tilt_bob'` and exactly one has `'slam'`, and anything else throws as a `TABLE` authoring defect rather than degrading. That helper is private to `sim/physics/cabinet/index.ts` and `sim/rules` must not import from `sim/physics` (AD-1); the rules-side consumer needs its own derivation of the same shape. Sibling precedents: `sim/loop`'s `buttonSwitchByAction()`, `sim/physics/plunger.ts`'s `findManualPlungeDevice()`, `ball-controller.ts`'s `shooterLaunchCoil()`.
- **`HARDWARE_COILS` structurally EXCLUDES `c_autolaunch`, so AC 2's "and autolaunch" cannot be met by that batch.** `hardwareCoils()` is the complement of every coil a ball device names as its eject coil or a `pulse` step in its `ballSearchOrder`, minus the drop-bank reset coil — and `c_autolaunch` is `bd_shooter`'s own `ballSearchOrder` pulse step. The exclusion is deliberate and measured: a `disable` for a serving coil lands in the **same `coilCommands` batch** as the controller's own serve pulse and swallows it (DW-74's same-tick-wins-disable precedent, pinned by `test/coil-enable.test.ts`). What already suppresses the autolaunch while tilted is the ball controller's own `!nextState.machine.tilt.tilted` guard around the deferred-launch pulse. **So 2.11 must satisfy "autolaunch disabled" through the rules-side guard and record that reasoning, not by emitting a `disable` for `c_autolaunch`.** The `disable`-set pin itself has a precedent to follow: DW-181 replaced a `length > 0` assertion with a loop over `HARDWARE_COILS` asserting each coil receives one.
- **`DW-222` is the sharpest thing 2.11 inherits, and it lives three lines from that guard.** The deferred-autolaunch arrival branch sets `awaitingSaveLaunch = false` **before** the `!tilt.tilted` check, so the flag is spent whether or not the coil fires. That placement is **deliberate** — the 2.9 implement-stage review added it to stop a later arrival double-firing — so any fix must preserve the anti-double-fire property. The consequence once `machine.tilt.tilted` can become true: a Tilt engaging between the save's `c_trough_eject` and the re-served ball's arrival at `bd_shooter` leaves the ball resting in the shooter lane with **`ballsInPlay` stuck at 0, no `ball_ended`, no `ball_will_start` and no recovery** until ball search lands in 2.12. **2.11 both makes it reachable and owns what a Tilt should do to a ball already saved.** Its sibling `DW-230` (`awaitingSaveLaunch` never got the bounded lifetime `awaitingSaveRelaunch` has, and still carries a justification comment a later rework disproved) is `wontfix-accepted` on the *same field* — if 2.11's fix gives that latch a bounded lifetime, DW-230 closes as a side effect and should be adjudicated rather than left standing.
- **2.11 is `disarmBallSave()`'s first production caller, and "Tilt disarms all" is not what that function does.** It removes **one** source: an unknown source is a no-op returning the same object, and `untilTick` returns to `null` only once `sources` is empty — never recomputed to a shorter surviving source's window (`DW-219`, routed to 3.7, whose "unreachable in Epic 2" basis is *one source, zero production disarm callers*; 2.11 removes half that premise, so file a trailer). With exactly one source today a disarm does empty `sources` and reset `untilTick`, so the bug does not bite — but AD-18's clause is "Tilt disarms **all**", and the code comment in `ball-save.ts` explicitly distinguishes the whole-device `EMPTY_BALL_SAVE` grace-lapse reset from "Story 2.11's later per-source Tilt `disarm()`". Decide which shape you mean and say why. Note also that `machine.ballSave` is **frozen at exactly `{ untilTick, sources }`** inside the hashed `GameState`, so widening it moves `expectedHash`/`expectedGameStateHash` on all five goldens.
- **What 2.11 will find already built, so it consumes rather than creates.** `machine.tilt` exists as `TiltState { tilted, slamTilted }`, machine-scoped, booted `false` in both `sim/loop` and `startBall`, and **already reset by `ball_will_start`** — AC 3's clearing half is done. `players[i].tiltWarnings` exists, player-scoped, inside the hashed state. `GameAdjustments.tiltWarnings` exists on the replay contract. **All four tilt/slam tunables already exist in `TUNING`**, top-level, each carrying `source` and `confidence` and each already listed in the ratchet: `tiltWarningSpacingMs` **500**, `tiltSettleMs` **3000**, `slamNudgesPerWindow` **3**, `slamNudgeWindowMs` **500** — all `unverified`, all authored because no artifact states a figure. Two read-side tilt guards already ship and become reachable only now: Story 2.9's ball-save disarm/block (`!tilt.tilted` on both the save re-serve and the deferred autolaunch) and `lampsOf`'s `l_ball_save` inertness while tilted; Story 2.10's bonus forfeit (`total` forced to 0, and the tilted branch must clear `pendingBonusCountSteps` rather than arm — see vacuity #51). **`l_ball_save` is the ONLY tilt-aware lamp**; the other fourteen inserts project unchanged under Tilt, which is undecided rather than decided.
- **`DW-36` is 2.11's second inherited entry and it is a tunable question, not a comment fix.** FR-14's default of 1 was never transcribed: `tuning.ts` seeds the spacing and settle windows but **no `tiltWarnings`**, even though AD-15 names `tiltWarnings` among the table tunables. Whichever home it gets, **AD-14 binds the behaviour**: sim adjustments layer table default → preset (empty in v1) → player override and **apply at the next game**, so a mid-game change must not take effect. If it becomes a `TUNING` entry it is a *new tunable* and pays the costs in the next two bullets.
- **AD-15's tunable gate is now RATCHETED, and a new tunable that misses it reddens.** `test/tuning.test.ts` used to check a hand-maintained `scalarKeys` allowlist; Story 2.10's review found **ten** top-level entries escaping the `source`/`confidence` check entirely (2.10's four bonus tunables, 2.9's three ball-save timers, and three geometry figures never listed at all) — the same miss the file already recorded happening once before for `lockEjectExemptionTimeoutMs`. So the class was fixed, not the instance: a completeness ratchet now derives the declared set **from `TUNING` itself** and asserts it equals `scalarKeys`, with a `> 20` sanity floor so it cannot pass on an empty set. `scalarKeys` holds **31** entries. **A new tunable must therefore be built with `entry(value, source, confidence)` — non-empty string `source`, `confidence` in `high | medium | low | unverified` — AND be listed by name in `scalarKeys`.** The pre-existing naming rules still apply on top: `…Ms` only for durations and only top-level (a nested `…Ms` key throws at load), no unit suffix for scoring values.
- **The golden budget, and how Story 2.10 actually paid it.** `tableHash()` hashes the **whole frozen `TABLE`** in canonical JSON, so any new lamp, switch, device or wiring key moves it — 2.10 added `TABLE.bonusWiring` and `tableHash` went `bac5816c → 8838dd46`. `resolveTuning()`'s **entire serialized output** — `source` prose and `confidence` included — is compared as whole-tree canonical JSON, so **a new tunable, or a prose-only provenance correction, moves `header.gameStart.tuning` and invalidates all five goldens before tick 1** even when `TABLE` never moves; a `…Ms` tunable adds **two** blocks (itself plus its derived `…Ticks` sibling, which copies `source`/`confidence` verbatim). `assetHash` hashes the **parsed collision document only** and stayed `ab163ff`. **None of the five goldens presses `s_start`** — all stay in `phase: 'attract'`, no mode is pushed, `rng` never advances, `players[]` stays empty — which is why a wiring-and-tuning change is header-only by construction. **A header-only refresh is routine and needs no author grant** (precedents 2.4, 2.9, 2.10). What needs the grant is a **trajectory** re-record. **A moved *state* hash means something other than a header change happened: HALT and report, do not re-record around it.**
- **How to re-record, and how to prove you did not move a body.** There is no recording tool — build the harness **in the scratchpad, never under `test/`** (a file matching `test/**/*.test.ts` silently joins `pnpm test`), over the shipped `runReplay` / `buildHeader` / `tableHash()` / `assetHash(doc)` / `PHYSICS_VERSION` surface. **Verify structurally, by parsing JSON per field — never by substring grep** — and **append** to each `notes` rather than rewriting; an unanchored `git diff | grep` over golden field names now matches the appended `notes` prose and emits false HALTs, so anchor any pre-filter to the field's own key position (`^[-+]\s*"<field>"`). Preserve `gameStart.tuning`'s **declaration** key order. Story 2.10 shipped the per-field structural diff plus an explicit **identity argument** in each `notes` (the new code paths are unreachable from any recorded trajectory) in place of a trace diff, and pinned it with an AC-9 mutation: reverting one golden's `tableHash` reddened exactly the six tests scoped to that golden with the named `StaleReplayHeaderError`, leaving the other four green.
- **`DW-208` is CLOSED, and how it closed is the load-bearing part.** The ledger entry prescribed wiring `modeEvents` **through the loop**; that is barred, because `test/ad7-device-slots.test.ts` permits exactly two writes to `sim/loop`'s `state` binding and the multiplier is `GameState`, so a forwarding-only channel would have been a fifth `FrameOutput` channel with no reader — the very vacuity the entry existed to prevent. Disabled, they act as passive rubber and emit no actuation. **The bob is a real pendulum**: `createCabinetMechanics()` emits an `s_tilt_bob` edge each time `isOverThreshold` changes, so one nudge burst produces a *sequence* of closures as the bob oscillates and decays — Story 1.7 measured the longest consecutive over-threshold run at **15 ticks** for its burst, which is why a 200-tick hold is not reachable and why the debounce must be a semantic rules window rather than a switch setting (`settleClass` is 0 ms for both `tilt_bob` and `slam`). **The slam detector is a separate tick-windowed nudge count that shares nothing with the bob** — no threshold, no state — and Story 1.7's suite proves that independence is observable at the emitting seam, not merely true by reading the source.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- **One fixed authored camera, no camera controls.** Both flippers, the Dragon, both Loops, the Ramp, the DRAGON bank and the backbox strip carrying the Backglass must all be legible from it.
- **The Backglass is a low-resolution dot-matrix display** — a visible dot grid, no smoothing, **128×32 and 1-bit** — showing every player's score with the current player highlighted, the ball number, and the highest-priority mode's name and whichever fields it publishes, converted to display units in presentation. **`DmdScreen` is a closed union and an explicit contract, not an implementation detail** — its own comment already names 2.11's TILT as a future member alongside 2.13's final scores and Match, so adding it is anticipated work, not a surprise. **Its vertical budget is the live constraint and the author has decided the fix:** with four players the BALL row drops; at three or more the whole mode block drops; at two the mode *name* survives and only its optional fields drop. The warning display 2.11 owes must be planned against that budget, not on top of it.
- **A pinning test for a rendered row must assert the RENDERED DOTS DIFFER, never merely that a field is set.** This is Story 2.6's standing precedent, it is a binding condition on Story 2.13's QA gate for `DmdRow.emphasis` (written and asserted, but **no renderer reads it**, so the panel never identifies *which* player a score belongs to), and Story 2.10 reproduced the failure it exists to prevent — vacuity #50, where a whole-buffer `some(d => d === 1)` predicate would have shipped a blank BONUS row green. Use the differential band form: derive the band from the row's **own** declared coordinate, require it non-empty where the row should appear and **empty on a control frame** where it should not.
- **The mode line shows a mode or nothing at all** — the base mode's internal identifier must never leak to the panel, and the fix is pinned on the rasterised dot buffer rather than on a helper's return value. **Its residual is live:** the fix generalises past the decision, so any unlabelled mode now suppresses its published fields too. Harmless at this tree, because base is the only unlabelled mode.
- **The colour grammar is fixed, never varies, and is fully delivered across fifteen inserts:** white for a lit or qualifying shot, orange for DRAGON letters and the Lock, red for Hurry-up, green for Quick multiball, blue for Joust, purple for extra ball and special. Progression within a colour is a brightness and blink ladder expressed as the step value, with cadence timed by presentation and no command traffic from rules per blink. **What is deferred is only the dynamic light's *visible* share of it**, and only until Epic 4 gives the scene headroom.
- **Flipper buttons move the lit insert** one position per press across the Top lanes and across the inlane/outlane set, wrapping. **Per the author's decision, lane change matters before the plunge, not during** — and the skill shot closes on the **first** playfield closure of any kind at or after `ball_launched`, with an **unlit** Top lane a MISS rather than a skip. **The shipped code satisfies that operative rule but not yet its rationale**, and that gap is `DW-204`, **decision-pending and explicitly coupled to Story 2.14**. A second composition question sits beside it as `DW-232`: the skill shot survives a ball save, so a saved ball gets a second attempt from an *automatic* launch with no player aim. **Neither is 2.11's to decide, and both touch tilt-adjacent surfaces — fence them explicitly the way Story 2.10's spec did.**
- **Presentation never joins a tick-*t* event to a later snapshot.** An end-of-ball screen names the player from the event payload, never from the snapshot that has already advanced. Two decision-pending entries live on exactly that screen and should be decided **together**, by the author, not by a story stage: **`DW-236`** — the bonus counts *up* while FR-20 and AD-3 both name a count-*down*, with no scoring difference either way; and **`DW-237`** — the frozen score line already contains the bonus from its first frame, so the BONUS row counts up to a total the line above has already shown (the fix, freezing `score − ballEndedEvent.total` and advancing per step, needs no new field).
- **Attract in this epic is minimal**: the Backglass cycles the last game's scores with a start prompt and shows the flipper, plunge and Start keys once from the view configuration. The camera walk-up belongs to Epic 4. **Slam tilt's "return to Attract" lands on this same minimal Attract**, and Story 2.13 owns the general game-over → Attract transition — so 2.11 should reach that state, not build a second path to it.
