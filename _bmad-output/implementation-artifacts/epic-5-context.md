# Epic 5 Context: Art Passes — the Dragon and the Table

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

Turn the bare scene into a table a player can see and read. Story 5.0a (done) gave every visible collision body and moving mechanism a flat-shaded `vis_` placeholder twin. The remaining passes replace those twins behind the same node names with v1 art. The art is pipeline-generated and stylized: geometry and painted-look textures are generated by script. The passes cover mechanisms, plastics, ramp and guides that read as hardware; playfield art under saturated insert lenses; dot-matrix Backglass art that sets up the dragon-and-knight fiction; the cabinet and backbox at real scale; and the Dragon. Art may trail epics 1–4 and never blocks them. Each pass lands with its attribution entries. Because everything sits behind fixed node names, author-made art can replace it later without code changes.

## Stories

- Story 5.0a: Visible placeholder geometry (done)
- Story 5.4: Mechanisms, plastics, ramp and guides
- Story 5.2: Playfield art and materials
- Story 5.5: Backglass art and DMD frames
- Story 5.3: Cabinet, backbox and glass
- Story 5.1: The Dragon

Build in this order: 5.4 → 5.2 → 5.5 → 5.3 → 5.1.

## Requirements & Constraints

- **Stylized at real dimensions.** Use simplified geometry and stylized painted-look textures, generated by the pipeline (or author-painted), with real proportions and real lighting behaviour. Photoreal detail, high triangle counts and high texture resolution are not goals.
- **Triangle budget:** one LOD per object, 500–2,000 triangles (the Dragon ≤ 2,000). No LOD chains.
- **Legibility:** from the one fixed view, a player must be able to make out both flippers, the Dragon, both Loops, the Ramp and the DRAGON bank. The Backglass stays visible as a strip at the top.
- **Mechanisms read as hardware.** Rubber posts and bands must look like rubber. The ball's visible path must match its collision path, so no ball ever passes through a visual.
- **Inserts** are saturated lights under lenses and cups set into a translucent playfield, never decals. No muted or earth-tone inserts. Every insert must read clearly against the art at the fixed camera distance, including which Top lane is lit.
- **Dragon:** a stylized creature crouched off-centre over its Lock lane, with a mouth.
- **Cabinet:** the cabinet, backbox, glass, legs and lockdown bar have standard-body dimensions and are framed by the fixed camera.
- **Backglass:** dot-matrix frame sequences at the Backglass resolution for Attract, Mode prompts, the War start, the Jackpot and Match. The score must stay legible during and after every sequence.
- **Performance and size:** stable 60 FPS on a 2022 mid-range laptop GPU. The compressed initial payload is ≤ 20 MB, enforced in CI. Texture memory and lighting are the real costs, not geometry.
- **Provenance is a hard gate.** Every file gets an `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` entry *before* it lands.
  - Record generated files as generated, naming the tool and date (Claude Code, Blender 5.2.1).
  - v1 uses no third-party asset. If `pinball-parts` or any other external asset is ever used, verify its CC BY-SA licence at source and exclude its NC-SA node group.
  - Verify any font's licence at source.
  - No NC, unlicensed or GPL-2.0-only material, and nothing from commercial machines.
  - New source files carry the GPL-3.0 header.
- **Human-only checks are author action items, not story gates.** These are the Dragon's Lawlor feel-test re-run and the SM-3 stranger link test for the war-fiction Attract sequence. Stories close on everything the pipeline can verify.

## Technical Decisions

- **The Blender source owns all geometry.** `assets/src/dragonwar.blend` owns every position, mesh and switch zone, and `tools/make-placeholder-blend.py` generates it.
  - `tools/export.py` validates the source and writes `public/assets/dragonwar.glb` (presentation) and `dragonwar.collision.json` (physics).
  - The validator checks node names (`^[a-z][a-z0-9_]*$`, unique, one material each) and every `lightgroup`, `surface` and `phys_material` against a dump of `TABLE`.
  - Both loaders fail fast on a missing node or an unknown property.
- **Node contract.** Art replaces visuals behind the same names.
  - `col_` is invisible collision and the only thing the ball hits. `sw_` is switch zones. `vis_` is non-collidable visuals. `l_` is inserts, with lens and cup below the surface. Mechanisms are named as their device.
  - The glb has exactly two top-level nodes, `playfield_root` and `cabinet_root`, plus `pivot_pitch`.
  - Static meshes carry `TEXCOORD_1` (lightmap UV) and a `lightgroup`.
  - The playfield material carries a translucency mask that matches every `l_` position.
- **Visible placeholders (from 5.0a).** Each player-visible `col_<x>` has a `vis_<x>` twin, copied from it in the same generator run.
  - The four `surface: 'dragon'` bodies merge into one `vis_dragon`.
  - These get no twin: `col_playfield` (it has `vis_playfield`), `col_glass` (Story 5.3) and bodies under the apron.
  - A twin's material family comes from its body's `surface`. The exception is the non-drop `target` backstop, which takes the wall family.
  - The name helpers live in `src/presentation/scene/vis-names.ts`.
  - A `vis_` twin never enters the collision document, `tableHash` or `assetHash`.
- **Moving visuals follow the snapshot.** `src/presentation/mechanisms/` poses flippers, drop targets, the spinner and the plunger each frame from `Snapshot.mechanisms`, with no interpolation and never from sim state.
- **Plunger travel (DW-292).** The snapshot's plunger position is currently hard-wired to 0. Story 5.4 may fix `src/sim/physics/plunger.ts`, the one sanctioned `src/sim` edit in this epic, only if every golden's `expectedHash` and `expectedGameStateHash` stays unchanged.
- **Dragon jaw contract.** The Dragon mesh carries a named jaw node, a child of `vis_dragon`, with its pivot at the hinge. `col_dragon_body`, `sw_lock_lane` and `bd_lock` keep their names, or are re-authored to the same names. The rig is driven only by `show_dragon_mouth_open` and the hit-reaction shows. This epic creates no `src/presentation/mechanisms/dragon.ts`.
- **Frames:** geometry is authored unpitched in the table frame (mm, right-handed, origin bottom-left, Y up the playfield, Z toward the glass). Pitch rotates only `playfield_root`, about `pivot_pitch`; `cabinet_root` stays level. Only the three conversions in `frames.ts` convert units or axes.
- **Lighting:**
  - Each insert is emissive plus a dynamic light beneath a transmissive lens, behind `LampDriver`. Tune insert intensity so the role colour survives rather than clipping to white.
  - The `gi_backbox`, `gi_cabinet` and `gi_arch` channels light the cabinet art and the backbox translucency.
  - A later per-group bake must not require renaming meshes.
- **Backglass:**
  - Sequences are keyed by plain event-name strings in a presentation-side map in `presentation/backglass`, and are selected and timed there from payload-complete events.
  - English literals live only in `presentation/backglass`.
  - This epic wires and verifies only Attract and Match.
- **Boundaries:**
  - `presentation/**` imports only `sim/contracts` and `sim/table`.
  - Device-name string literals outside `sim/table/dragonwar.ts` and `test/**` are lint errors.
- **Hashes:** a visual-only change must not move any physics or rules state hash. A header-only re-record of the goldens' asset hash is expected when the model changes. `NullEngine` draws nothing, so any claim that something is visible needs a real browser capture.
- **Conventions:** `assets/src/` holds editable sources and `public/assets/` holds exported files. File names are `kebab-case.ts`. Only `host/` logs.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- **5.0a's twins are the base.** Stories 5.1–5.4 replace them behind the same `vis_` names.
- **Checks moved to other epics, not to be rebuilt here:**
  - The Dragon rig playing on the new mesh → Story 3.3b (Epic 3, after Epic 5 merges).
  - The Walk-up framing the backbox and descending past the glass → Story 4.6.
  - Real-event triggers for the Mode-prompt, War-start and Jackpot sequences → Story 4.5.
- **Story 5.2 and Story 4.2 (inserts through cups)** must agree on lens positions and the translucency mask.
- **Story 5.5** builds on Story 2.6's DMD renderer.
- **Epic 3 runs in parallel.** Both epics re-record golden replay headers (`test/replays/**`) and contend for `src/sim/table`, so coordinate those edits.
- **Routed geometry defects may need `col_`/`sw_` edits.** Any such edit moves physics hashes, so re-record the goldens deliberately.
  - Story 5.4: DW-142 (a bare shooter-lane divider end), DW-258 (a ball sinking at the Ramp entrance).
  - Story 5.2: DW-161 (pop-skirt switch zones overlap), DW-159 (the post-protrusion gate).
  - Story 5.1: DW-165 (reconcile `col_dragon_body` with the committed geometry).
