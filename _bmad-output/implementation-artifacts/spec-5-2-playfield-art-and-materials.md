## Intent

**Problem:**
- The playfield is a flat brown slab. Its "translucency mask" is a uniform 16×16 image at alpha 0.5, so it matches no lens, and it dilutes every insert and the whole deck 50 % toward the background.
- Every insert shares one pale lens (`mat_insert`, albedo 0.9, 0.9, 0.95). The lead measured lit against unlit on `l_top_2` at a difference of about 11 levels, so a player cannot see which Top lane is lit (DW-271).
- `sw_pop_1`/`sw_pop_3` and `sw_pop_2`/`sw_pop_3` overlap by 26×6 mm, so one ball can fire two pop coils on one tick (DW-161).

**Approach:**
- `tools/make-placeholder-blend.py` generates a deterministic, stylized painted-look playfield texture, with no third-party input. Its RGB is the art, and its alpha is a binary translucency mask with an opening at exactly each `l_` lens.
- The script gives `vis_playfield` a planar UV.
- It replaces the pale lens with a dark lens tinted by the insert's home role.
- It splits the facing edges of the pop switch zones.
- Everything stays behind the fixed names (`vis_playfield`, `mat_playfield`, `img_playfield_translucency`, `l_*`), so author art can replace it later without code changes.
- A dev hatch lets the lead light any insert through the real `LampDriver` for the browser check.

  - nothing was sourced.

- **Texture.** It is one RGBA image named `img_playfield_translucency`, 512×1024 px (about 1 px/mm), generated by numpy inside Blender with a fixed seed.
  - The same script run must give the same PNG bytes in the glb.
  - Its PNG is at most 512 KiB (524,288 B), the ceiling this story sets itself.
  - `pnpm check:size` stays green, and the measured before and after figures are recorded in `## Verification`.
  - If the ceiling cannot be met, reduce the noise first. Dropping to 256×512 is allowed only if recorded.
- **Material.** `mat_playfield` keeps its name and its alpha-from-image wiring. The image's Color drives Base Color and its Alpha drives Alpha.
- **Mesh contract.** `vis_playfield` keeps its name, its parent, `TEXCOORD_1` (`uv_lightmap`) and `lightgroup: 'lg_playfield'`. Its `uv_base` top face maps table (x, y) affinely and axis-aligned onto [0, 1]².
- **Mask derivation.** The mask is derived in the same run from the same `(cx, cy, half_lens)` values each `add_insert(...)` call receives, never re-typed. [AMENDED 2026-09-30, lead spec gate -- author decision] `add_insert(...)` records every lens it creates in one generator-level lens list, and the mask pass iterates THAT list -- never a hard-coded set of today's 15 -- so an `l_` lens added later (Epic 3's Story 3.3c adds Hurry-up, Quick multiball, Joust, Ramp, both Loops, War and Jackpot-ladder inserts after Epic 5 merges) opens the mask automatically.   - Alpha is 0 for every texel whose centre lies inside a lens rectangle inset by 1 texel.
  - Alpha is 255 for every texel whose centre lies at least 1 texel outside every lens rectangle.
  - Anything in between may be intermediate.
  - Result: exactly 15 openings, one per `TABLE.lamps` insert.
- **Art.** Stylized painted look in a rustic register that tells the dragon-and-knight war: soft-edged flat colour regions with low-amplitude brush-like noise, at a feature scale of at least 5 mm (the camera shows about 0.8 px/mm).
  - Example motifs: a wood-grain ground; a dragon silhouette and flame shapes near the Dragon and Lock; shield or lance motifs; painted arrows up the shot lanes; a dark outline ring around each lens opening.
  - Drawn from procedural shapes only, with no text.
  - It is not a flat fill: the linear-luminance standard deviation of the opaque texels is at least 0.02.
- **Parts stay legible against the art** (this replaces 5.4's rule against `mat_playfield`'s flat colour). Take each flipper, drop-target, ramp and guide art part (the `ART_LEGIBLE_KINDS`), and `vis_dragon`. Take the opaque texels in the ring 2–12 mm outside the part's `col_` footprint(s). The part's material differs from their mean linear colour by at least 0.25 in some channel.
- **Lens.** Each `l_` node's material is a dark lens tinted by its home role, the role `lampsOf()` lights it in today:
  - `lane` and `ball_save` subjects use `lit`, in material `mat_insert`;
  - `letter` and `lock` subjects use `dragon`, in material `mat_insert_dragon`.

  Base colour = `LAMP_GRAMMAR[homeRole] × LENS_TINT_LEVEL`, where `LENS_TINT_LEVEL = 0.08` (the lead's measured envelope). It is one constant in the script with a comment citing `grammar.ts`, and the test pins proportionality against the live `LAMP_GRAMMAR`. No texture, and metallic 0. Lens and cup geometry are unchanged.

  - No `col_` node changes.
