## Intent

**Problem:** Nothing in the shipped product can set `machine.tilt.tilted`. Physics already emits both cabinet-sensor switches — `createCabinetMechanics()` level-tracks the plumb bob's `isOverThreshold` and the slam detector's windowed nudge count and pushes `s_tilt_bob` / `s_slam_tilt` edges into the same `switchEvents` array `sim/loop` hands `rules.step()` (`src/sim/physics/cabinet/index.ts:204-207, 248-255`) — but the devices layer's `buildPlayfieldSwitches()` **excludes both settle classes by construction** (`src/sim/rules/devices/index.ts:189`) and no branch of its six-stage dispatch (`devices/index.ts:249-384`) matches either switch, so both are **silently dropped** and emit no device event of any kind. AD-19 forbids anything outside `src/sim/rules/devices/` from reaching for a raw `SwitchEvent`, so no other layer can pick them up. The consequence is a whole band of already-shipped read-side code that no test can reach in play: Story 2.9's ball-save disarm guard and deferred-autolaunch guard (`ball-controller.ts:676-678, 642`), Story 2.9's `l_ball_save` inertness while tilted (`lamps.ts:78-83`), and Story 2.10's bonus forfeit (`ball-controller.ts:694-695`) — Story 2.10's own record says its tilted ball was *"only ever seeded via `initialState`, never reached in play"*. `SemanticEvent` (`src/sim/contracts/events.ts:246-260`) carries no tilt member; `DmdScreen` (`src/presentation/backglass/frame.ts:62`) carries no TILT screen, though its own comment already names one as this story's; `disarmBallSave()` (`src/sim/rules/ball-save.ts:73-79`) has **zero** production callers; `tiltWarningSpacingTicks` and `tiltSettleTicks` resolve on every `resolveTuning()` and are read by **nothing**; and `GameAdjustments.tiltWarnings` has no table default at all (`DW-36`), even though AD-15's own Rule names `tiltWarnings` in `sim/table/tuning.ts`'s inventory.

**Approach:** Give the two cabinet sensors a device-event seam and put one owner behind it. `src/sim/rules/devices/index.ts` gains a rules-side structural derivation of the two switch names from their unique `SettleClass` — mirroring the file-private `switchNameForSettleClass()` physics already uses (`cabinet/index.ts:118-126`), which `sim/rules` may not import (AD-1) — and Stage 3's closed-edge loop emits two new `DeviceEvent`s, `tilt_bob_closed` and `slam_tilt_closed`. `PLAYFIELD_SWITCHES` is untouched: a bob closure is neither "the ball reached the playfield" nor "has anything closed?". A new `src/sim/rules/tilt.ts` holds `createTiltController(adjustments, tuning)`, constructed by `createRules()` alongside the ball controller and stepped from `rules.step()` **before** the ball controller so a Tilt on tick *t* is already true for every guard the controller runs on tick *t*. It owns the two semantic windows (`tiltWarningSpacingMs` for eligibility, `tiltSettleMs` for the warning cool-down) as closure state — never `GameState`, because `machine.tilt` is present in all five goldens' attract snapshots and widening it is a state-hash re-record — writes `players[currentPlayer].tiltWarnings`, `machine.tilt`, `machine.hardwareEnabled` and (through `disarmBallSave`, AD-18's own named interface) `machine.ballSave`, and emits `tilt_warning { player, remaining }`, `tilt { player }`, `slam_tilt` plus the `CoilCommand disable` batch. `DW-36` is closed by transcribing FR-14's default into `TUNING.tiltWarnings` — the one place AD-15 names — and having both `DEFAULT_ADJUSTMENTS` and `src/host/boot.ts`'s real `GameStart` read it, so the figure has exactly one definition. The Backglass gains two `DmdScreen` members driven by the two different truths AD-9 allows: the transient warning from the event, the TILT condition from the snapshot.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- `src/sim/rules/devices/` stays the only consumer of `SwitchEvent` (AD-19, enforced textually by `tools/boundary-lint.mjs`'s `rules-no-switch-event-outside-devices` check at `:661-771`). The tilt controller consumes `DeviceEvent`s and never a switch.
- The two switch names are derived **structurally from their unique `SettleClass`**, never typed as literals: `DEVICE_NAME_PATTERN` (`tools/boundary-lint.mjs:97`) matches `s_tilt_bob` and `s_slam_tilt`, and the only path-level exemption under `src/**` is `src/sim/table/dragonwar.ts` (`boundary-lint.mjs:560-565`). Exactly one `TABLE.switches` entry carries `settleClass: 'tilt_bob'` and exactly one `'slam'` (`dragonwar.ts:249-250`); anything else throws as a `TABLE` authoring defect rather than degrading (`cabinet/index.ts:118-126` and `ball-controller.ts:192-198` are the two in-tree shapes).
- `sim/rules/**` never imports from `sim/physics/**` (AD-1's dependency graph). Note this direction has **no** dependency-cruiser or boundary-lint rule today — it holds by convention and by the fact that nothing does it — so the rules-side derivation is authored independently rather than by reaching for the physics helper (which is file-private in any case).
- `GameState` mutates only inside `rules.step()` (AD-7), gated at source by `test/ad7-device-slots.test.ts:445-489`, which permits exactly two writes to `sim/loop`'s `state` binding. `tiltWarnings` is player-scoped; `tilt`, `hardwareEnabled` and `ballSave` are machine-scoped.
- Every folding stage returns the **same `GameState` reference** when nothing changed (`src/sim/rules/bonus.ts:138-146, 187-195` is the precedent, and `rules/index.ts:229-234` records why structural sharing is load-bearing for DW-70's identity gate).
- Both semantic windows are read through the resolved tuning, at production magnitude, and every test offset is authored as a literal at the probe — never read back from the tunable (epic vacuity #43; Story 2.9 shipped `ballSaveGraceMs` and `ballSaveHurryUpMs` each settable to `1` with the whole suite green, twice).
- Every window mark the tilt controller holds is **reset-safe against a restarted timeline**: `src/host/loop.ts`'s `reset()` (tuning-panel hot-apply, replay playback, the two dev hatches in `boot.ts`) rebuilds the sim with `createLoop()` and restarts `tick` at 0 while the surrounding closures survive. `src/presentation/backglass/frame.ts:180-192` records the same failure once already — a stale deadline froze the panel for minutes. A recorded mark strictly greater than the current `tick` is a mark from a different timeline and must be discarded, not compared against.
- `TUNING.tiltWarnings` is a top-level `entry(value, source, confidence)` with a non-empty `source` and a `confidence` from `high | medium | low | unverified`, **and** is listed by name in `test/tuning.test.ts`'s `scalarKeys` — the completeness ratchet at `test/tuning.test.ts:92-108` derives the declared set from `TUNING` itself and reddens on any unlisted top-level entry. It carries **no** unit suffix (a count, following `skillShotAward` at `tuning.ts:637-648`), and therefore derives no `…Ticks` sibling.
- Every drain-driving test runs at a **non-saving** tuning (`NO_BALL_SAVE_TUNING`, five byte-identical file-local definitions) or seeds the device disarmed, and asserts `ball_ended` actually arrived. A live ball save returns from `ball-controller.ts:680-685` before the entire end-of-ball path — "a parking entry taking `ballsInPlay` to 0 ends the ball" is false; there are two exits.
- Any harness built to refresh the goldens lives in the scratchpad, never under `test/` (a file matching `test/**/*.test.ts` silently joins `pnpm test`).

**Block If:**
- Any golden's `expectedHash`, `expectedGameStateHash`, `expectedCheckpointHashes`, `checkpointTicks`, `transitions`, `coilPrologue`, `durationTicks`, `header.assetHash`, `header.tableHash`, `header.physicsVersion`, `header.tickHz`, `header.physicsSeed`, or `header.gameStart.{seed,adjustments,highscores}` moves. - Any change to `TABLE` would be required. `tableHash()` hashes the whole frozen `TABLE` (`replay.ts:145-147`); this story's seam is derived from switches that already exist, so `header.tableHash` must stay `8838dd46`.
- The implementation would require `src/sim/loop/**` to write any `GameState` field, or a third write to its `state` binding.
- `HARDWARE_COILS`'s derivation (`ball-controller.ts:163-172`) would have to be widened to include `c_autolaunch`. It is the complement of every ball-serving coil precisely so a `disable` cannot land in the same `coilCommands` batch as a serve pulse and swallow it (`DW-74`, pinned by `test/coil-enable.test.ts:77-88`). See `## Design Notes`, "Why AC 3's autolaunch clause is met by the guard, not the batch".
- The suite shows a red `check:ad7`, `check:corridor` or `check:reachability`. Every gate is expected green; a red one is a regression, not the status quo.

**Never:**
- Never emit a device event for `s_tilt_bob` or `s_slam_tilt` through `playfield_switch_closed`, and never add either switch to `PLAYFIELD_SWITCHES`. Its membership is **derived by subtraction** and is Story 2.12's answer to "has anything closed?" and Story 2.9's to "did the ball reach the playfield?"; a bob closure is neither. The set stays at **28** switches.
- Never reset the tilt bob by command, and never add a command or event that does (AD-7's Rule states this explicitly: *"The bob is never reset by command — its physical decay plus `tiltSettleMs` is the settle"*).
- Never let an uppercase `TILT` or `WARNING` appear anywhere under `src/sim/**`, in identifiers or literals, outside a comment. `test/backglass-frame.test.ts:796-811`'s negative control does a plain `contents.includes(literal)` over comment-stripped source, so a constant named `TILT_…` in `sim/rules` would redden the AD-9 "rules never format text" gate. Name rules-side identifiers in `camelCase`/`snake_case` (`tiltWarningSpacingTicks`, `'tilt_warning'`). Measured at this tree: the only occurrence of an uppercase `TILT` anywhere under `src/sim/**` today is the word `UNTILTED` inside a doc comment at `src/sim/contracts/events.ts:202`, which `stripComments()` removes before the scan — so the gate is clean *now* and one careless constant would break it. `WARNING` has zero occurrences.
- Never add a `GameState` field, a lamp, a GI channel, a flasher or a show. Flashers on Tilt (FR-45) and the `show_tilt` audio cue (FR-48) are **Epic 4's**, not this story's.
- Never add a `tilt` phase to `GamePhase`. Tilt is a machine condition (`machine.tilt`), not a phase.
- Never make any lamp other than `l_ball_save` tilt-aware. It is today the only tilt-aware lamp (`lamps.ts:106-108`); whether the other fourteen inserts should go dark under Tilt is undecided, not decided, and is not this story's call.
- Never build a second game-over → Attract path. Story 2.13 owns the general transition; Slam tilt writes the minimal state change that reaches the same Attract (see `## Design Notes`).
- Never decide `DW-204` (does the paying skill-shot lane freeze at `ball_launched`), `DW-212` (`l_lock` between balls and in Attract), `DW-226` (ball-save eligibility after a grace lapse), `DW-232` (is a saved ball entitled to a second skill shot from an automatic launch), `DW-236` or `DW-237` (the end-of-ball screen's count direction and its score line). All six are `decision-pending` on the author's decision sheet. `DW-232` in particular is tilt-adjacent — a saved ball's second skill-shot attempt — and this story neither builds on it nor pre-empts it: nothing here reads or writes the skill-shot mode.
- Never re-own `DW-219` (disarm never shrinks `untilTick` to a surviving source's window). Its routing to Story 3.7 was re-confirmed on 2026-09-08 with a corrected premise: with a single arming source a disarm empties `sources` and resets `untilTick` correctly, so this story cannot reach it however many times it calls `disarm`.
- Never compute a test's expected value from `tiltWarningSpacingMs`, `tiltSettleMs` or `TUNING.tiltWarnings` by re-importing it. Never derive a non-vacuity guard's expected count from the helper it is guarding (vacuity #44).
- Never assert "no event is emitted" without first establishing, in the same test, that something *could* have been emitted (vacuity #51 — two Story 2.10 assertions held only because nothing had been armed earlier in those runs). This story is full of negative assertions: no coil fires, no bonus is paid, no autolaunch, no second warning.
