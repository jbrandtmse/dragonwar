## Intent

**Problem:** Story 2.1a proved a drain triangle beneath `y = 420 mm`; above that line the playfield is empty. Nothing exists for the Left and Right Loops, the Ramp, the off-centre Dragon with the Lock lane between its legs, the six-target DRAGON bank, the three Top lanes, the slingshots or the pop bumpers, and `TABLE.switches` still declares only Epic 1's eleven switches with `coils` at four and no `bd_lock`. Every later story in Epic 2 and Epic 3 lands on shots and switches that do not yet exist. Two switch classes the shot map introduces (`standup` 8 ms, `drop_target` 20 ms) also make ledger `DW-67` reachable for the first time: `createSwitchTracker` debounces the **make**, so a zone crossing shorter than `settleTicks + 1` ticks emits no edge at all -- which makes FR-11 ("no ball is ever lost by a missed switch at any ball speed the Physics core can produce") false by construction the moment the first drop target is declared.

**Approach:** Draw the rest of the shot map in `tools/make-placeholder-blend.py` above `GUIDE_Y_TOP_MM = 420`, meeting 2.1a's four `*_hi` rubber posts, and re-export; complete `TABLE.switches`, `TABLE.coils` and `TABLE.ballDevices.bd_lock`; place one `sw_` zone per zone-requiring switch; make the switch tracker latch on the make and debounce the break so a maximum-speed pass registers exactly once; close both directions of the registry/document validation; and record the two geometry decisions the epic charters to this story in a new `docs/decisions.md`. The seven-shot Lawlor ritual (FR-32) is author-owned and ships `pending-author`.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**

- Geometry is authored **only** by editing `tools/make-placeholder-blend.py` and re-running it headlessly, then `pnpm export:assets`. The `.blend` is a generated binary; every commit that ever changed it also changed that script, in lockstep. Blender is reached **only** through the `BLENDER` env var -- never write an executable path into a tracked source, config or test file.
- The `.glb`, the `.collision.json` and the `.blend` are regenerated and committed **together**. CI has no Blender and consumes the committed artifacts.
- Every figure this story authors above `y = 420` is an authored decision with **no source anywhere in the repository** (verified: the research digests contain no ramp, orbit, drop-target, pop, sling, Top-lane, post-radius or switch-zone dimension of any kind). - Every `col_`/`sw_` node is a MESH with an **identity object transform** -- angle is authored in mesh vertices, not in the object matrix (`export.py`'s rotation guard, `AXIS_ALIGNED_EPSILON = 1e-9`). `col_lane_deflector` is the prototype to copy in shape.
- Every wall footprint is **convex**. Decompose an L, U or notched shape into several convex `col_` nodes; a curve is a chain of convex prisms.
- Every guide's free end terminates at a node whose `surface` is `rubber_post`, never bare metal (FR-31, CAP-31, AD-11).

- Non-ASCII in source is authored as an escape sequence, never as a literal byte (Rule 14).
