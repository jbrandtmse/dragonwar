--- LEAD INTERPRETATION (epic runner, 2026-08-29) ---
Probe: 1000 Hz physics ticks (5000 ticks = 5 simulated seconds), committed collision
document, real loadCollision() + createFlipperMechanics(). Columns are RAW PHYSICS-frame
coordinates (not fromPhysics()-converted), so x/y are not table mm; only their TRENDS matter.
E = 0.5|v|^2 - (g . pos) = total mechanical energy per unit mass. Conserved under gravity alone.

1. ENERGY IS DISSIPATED, NOT INJECTED. E moves from -319.2 to -778.9 (held flipper) and from
   -128.8 to -947.3 (bare playfield) -- i.e. E becomes steadily MORE NEGATIVE in both runs.
   The solver is losing energy to friction/damping, which is the correct sign. There is no
   energy-injecting contact instability. (E/E0 reads > 1 only because E0 is negative; the
   ratio is increasing magnitude of a negative number, i.e. a decrease in E.)

2. THE RUNAWAY IS NOT FLIPPER-SPECIFIC. The control run never touches a flipper at all -- a
   ball simply placed on the bare playfield accelerates down-slope and exits the geometry on
   the SAME trajectory shape and reaches a HIGHER speed (56.3) than the held-flipper run
   (47.3). Whatever causes the departure is therefore not introduced by this story's port.

3. THE EXTREME DISPLACEMENT IS A HARNESS ARTIFACT. This diagnostic harness has no
   createDeviceMechanics(), so a ball that reaches the drain aperture is never captured by
   bd_trough -- it keeps free-falling down the 6.5 deg slope forever. "Ball ends up 4292 mm
   off the table" therefore means "the ball drained, and nothing in this harness caught it",
   not "the ball was flung off the playfield".

CONCLUSION: the ported FlipperMover/FlipperHit are not defective and the bat itself is
provably static while held. The ball departs the bat because the committed placeholder table
has NO geometry adjacent to either flipper -- the 12 collision nodes are the playfield, the
glass, five outer walls, two lane walls, the lane deflector and the two flipper bats. A real
cradle is a POCKET formed by the raised bat plus the inlane guide/post beside it; with no
such adjacent geometry the ball rolls along the bat under the 6.5 deg pitch and leaves it
after roughly 1.2-1.9 simulated seconds. That geometry is Epic 2's (Story 2.1, "The playfield
geometry and the full switch set"), and this story's Boundaries forbid touching the asset.
