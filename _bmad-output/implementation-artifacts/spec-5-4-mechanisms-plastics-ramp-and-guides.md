## Intent

**Problem:**
- Every visible part of the table is still a flat-shaded placeholder copied from its `col_` body (Story 5.0a), so the playfield reads as scaffolding (DW-249).
- The snapshot's `plunger.posMm` is hard-wired to 0, so the plunger never visibly pulls back (DW-292).
- `vis_spinner_l` is a stub with no pivot, and it sits inside the space the ball occupies.
- Two collision hazards were routed here:
  - DW-142: the shooter-lane divider has a bare, unterminated tip.
  - DW-258: a 24 mm slot at the Ramp entrance can wedge a ball, or sink one that was teleported into it.

**Approach:**
- `tools/make-placeholder-blend.py` gains an art pass. It replaces every `vis_` twin except `vis_dragon` with stylized parts, behind the same node names. Each part is derived in the same run from its `col_` body's footprint.
- The pass also re-authors the spinner and the plunger, and adds two sling plastics.
- Physics publishes the plunger's pull.
- The follower spins the spinner blade from `speed`.
- Two small `col_` bodies are added to seal DW-142 and DW-258. They move only `header.assetHash`.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- **Provenance.** Every regenerated file is generated by Claude Code with Blender 5.2.1 on 2026-09-29. That note goes into `ATTRIBUTIONS.md` before the file changes. No third-party asset, texture, image or glTF extension is added.
- **Art scope.** Every direct `vis_` child of `playfield_root` that is a 5.0a twin is replaced, except `vis_dragon` (Story 5.1). `vis_playfield`, `vis_backbox` and the `l_` inserts stay byte-for-byte as they are.
- **Naming.** A replaced part keeps its twin name, `visTwinName(col)` (the spine's "Visible placeholders" row), and stays a direct child of `playfield_root`.
- **Sub-parts.** A part that needs a second material gets child meshes named `<parent>_<part>` (for example `vis_flipper_l_rubber`).
- **Mesh contract.** Every mesh keeps AD-11/AD-12's contract: one material, `uv_base` plus `uv_lightmap` (TEXCOORD_1), and `lightgroup: 'lg_playfield'`.
- **Art materials.** Art materials are named `mat_art_<kind>`. They carry colour, roughness and metallic only, with no texture. `metallic` is 0 on all of them, because the scene has no environment map.
- **Derived, not re-typed.** Each art part's geometry comes from its source body's footprint within the same script run, for example a post's centre and radius, or a wall's polygon. Coordinates are never re-typed.
- **Ball band.** The ball band is table z `[0, TABLE.reference.ballMm]` (26.99 mm). Any triangle with a vertex inside the band keeps all of its vertices within 0.5 mm of its source body's `footprintMm` polygon. For an authored part with no source body, "within" means any `col_` footprint. The only exemption is `vis_plunger`, which has its own pins.
- **Height.** A part covering a body reaches at least `min(zHighMm, ballMm)`.
- **Bounding box.** Taken over the in-band portion of the whole node, a part's x/y bbox equals its body's bbox within 0.5 mm.
- **Parts above the ball.** Plastics, and every vertex of the spinner blade's swept circle, sit at z ≥ `ballMm + 1`.
- **Plastics and rubber.** No rubber-part vertex lies inside a plastic's xy footprint polygon.
- **Rubber.** Every `rubber_post` and `rubber_band` part carries a `mat_art_rubber` mesh with these properties:
  - it spans z = `ballMm / 2`;
  - its in-band xy bbox equals the body's bbox within 0.5 mm;
  - `mat_art_rubber` is roughness ≥ 0.8, metallic 0, and every linear base channel ≤ 0.10.
- **Legibility of materials.** The flipper, drop-target, ramp and guide art materials each differ from `mat_playfield`'s base colour `(0.45, 0.30, 0.15)` by at least 0.25 in at least one linear channel.
- **Triangle budget.** Each twin, counted with its descendants, has ≤ 2,000 triangles. Round parts use at most 16 segments with smooth normals. `pnpm check:size` stays green.
- **Moving parts.**
  - Flippers: the origin stays at the pivot, the authored pose is the end-of-stroke pose, and the bat mesh is symmetric about its centreline, because the follower reads the tip direction from the node's own bbox centre.
  - Drop targets: each stays a single mesh with identity rotation and scale.
  - `vis_plunger`: its centre x equals `bd_shooter`'s x, and its tip is at `bd_shooter.y − ballMm/2`.
- **Spinner (view state, AD-4).**
  - `vis_spinner_l` is a static bracket whose origin is on the spin axis, at y = `SPINNER_Y_MM`. Its child `vis_spinner_l_blade` rotates about `toScene(table +X)`.
  - The follower keeps a per-scene angle: `angleDeg += speed × Δtick / 1000`, where Δtick is the change in `snapshot.tick` since the last sync, and only when Δtick > 0.
  - `speed` 0 shows the rest pose, with the blade hanging toward table −Z.
  - Positive rotation goes about +X. The spin key comes from `Object.keys(TABLE.spinnerWiring)`.
- **DW-292.** `plunger.ts` reports `posMm = PLUNGER_STROKE_MM × t`.
  - `t` is exactly the clamped hold fraction that `plungerSpeedByHoldMs` computes (min/max hold ticks, with its zero-width guard). It is 0 once the plunger is released.
  - `PLUNGER_STROKE_MM = 38.1` (1.5 in) is an authored, display-only constant in `plunger.ts`, with a source comment. It follows the precedent of `SPINNER_AT_REST_DEG_PER_S`.
- **DW-142.** Add `col_post_wall_lane_cap`:
  - a `rubber_post` octagon, r 4, z 0–50, centred at (`LANE_X0_MM + WALL_T_MM/2`, `LANE_WALL_TOP_Y_MM − LANE_WALL_CAP_DROP_MM`) = (474.4, 944.0);
  - the FR-31 gate in `test/asset-contract.test.ts` brings `col_wall_lane` in **by name**, through a named inclusion record with a reason.
- **DW-258.** Add `col_ramp_slot_fill`:
  - surface/phys `plastic`, z 0–50;
  - footprint (366.4,485), (385.5613,485), (390.4,500), (390.4,750), (366.4,740), flush with `col_ramp_wall_r`, `col_loop_r_lower` and `col_loop_r_funnel`;
  - a `GUIDE_TERMINATION_EXEMPTIONS` entry with a shape reason and `verify()`.
- **Goldens.** Only `header.assetHash` changes, in all five goldens. `expectedHash`, `expectedGameStateHash`, `expectedCheckpointHashes` and `header.tableHash` stay unchanged, parsed per field.
- **Headers.** New source files carry the GPL-3.0 header.

**Never:**
- Edit `src/sim/table/**`, `src/sim/rules/**`, `src/sim/contracts/**`, or any `src/sim/**` file other than `plunger.ts`.
- Add a TUNING key or a TABLE field. Either one moves every golden header.
- Change, move or delete an existing `col_` or `sw_` body.
- Re-record a golden trajectory. If any trajectory field would move, HALT (`intent gap`) instead.
- Raise the visual Ramp above deck level. Collision has no inclined surface, so a raised ramp would make the ball pass through it visibly.
- Put a plastic over a flipper, the drop bank, the Ramp lane or a Loop lane.
- Weaken a gate. An assertion that 5.0a's placeholder pins remove must reappear as its art-contract equivalent.
