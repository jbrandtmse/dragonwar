# PRD Quality Review — DragonWar

Reviewed: `prd.md` and `addendum.md` (2026-08-26). Calibration: hobby / solo stakes; consumers are `bmad-architecture` and `bmad-create-epics-and-stories`. Technical "how" is intentionally in the addendum and is not penalised for being absent from the PRD body.

## Overall verdict

This is a good PRD. It has a real thesis ("one machine, built properly"; "being finished is the moat"), the Vision could not be swapped into another product, the Non-Goals and counter-metrics do actual work, and the FR-plus-Consequences format gives story creation something testable in most of the 55 FRs. What is at risk is concentrated in one place: the rules-layer interactions around the Lock in §4.6 — Hot seat with a physical Lock, War versus Quick multiball ball counts, where the Jackpot is collected, and what Tilt does mid-multiball. Those are the FRs that produce the headline moment, and they are the ones a story writer would currently have to invent. Ready for `bmad-architecture` as-is; run a short rules-interaction pass on §4.4–§4.6 before `bmad-create-epics-and-stories`.

## Decision-readiness — strong

Decisions are stated as decisions, not considerations: GPL-3.0 with the reason (§9), browser-first over the research's native-first recommendation (addendum §1, §5), independent Modes over campaign gating (§4.6 description; addendum §5 "Rejected 2026-08-26"), physical Lock over virtual (addendum §3), off-centre Dragon (FR-29), fixed camera (FR-2). The addendum's §5 table names what was given up for each, including things that were "Viable, not chosen" (Unity) rather than pretending every alternative was bad. The Open Questions are genuinely open — OQ1 (which Reference machine), OQ4 (Slam tilt trigger), OQ5 (mode-start device) each name the fallback without answering it. The single `[NOTE FOR PM]` (§6.2, wizard mode) sits at a real tension: "emotionally load-bearing" and deferred.

One tension is smoothed over rather than surfaced. The PRD commits to a physical Lock (FR-36, "holds up to two"), to per-player independent Lock count in Hot seat (FR-17), and to the fiction "the balls you locked are the balls that come back at you" (addendum §8). Those three cannot all be literally true with 1–4 players sharing one physical device, and the addendum §3 explicitly waves it away: "the real-machine reasons for virtual locks (BOM, trough reliability, per-player lock theft) do not apply to a sim." Lock theft is exactly the Hot-seat case. The sim can resolve it trivially (balls are free), but the rule has to be chosen.

### Findings
- **high** Hot seat × physical Lock is undecided (FR-17, FR-36, FR-37; addendum §3) — Player 1 locks two balls and drains; Player 2 has Lock count 0 and two balls physically in the Lock. When P2 lights the Lock and shoots the Lock lane, the device is at its stated capacity. Does it hold three, eject one (whose?), or does each player's Lock state become virtual with the physical balls as presentation? FR-17's "per-player state (… Lock count …) is independent" asserts the outcome without the mechanism, and the addendum's dismissal is wrong for this case. *Fix:* add a `[NOTE FOR PM]` and a decision: e.g. "Lock contents are per-player; on player change the Lock is physically re-stocked to that player's count (balls appear/disappear in the Lock during the player-change presentation)" — or "Lock is virtual for count, physical for eject: the Mouth always fires (locked-count + 1) balls." Then correct addendum §3.

## Substance over theater — strong

No persona theater: three JTBD lines, one primary, and each one drives something — the author drives SM-1/SM-2/SM-4 and the feel test, the stranger drives FR-53/FR-54/SM-3 and the "reads like a real machine" criterion, the virtual-pinball player drives "no ROM hunting" (§9, addendum §5 PinMAME row). No innovation theater — §1 says outright "The bet is not technical novelty. The field is empty, not contested." NFRs carry numbers (60 FPS, 480–1000 Hz, ≤ 16 ms, 10 s / 50 Mbps) and flag the ones that are guesses. The Vision is DragonWar-specific down to "backhand the Dragon with the left flipper." UJ-4 is unusual (a development ritual, not a journey) but it is the load-bearing acceptance mechanism for SM-4 and is used that way throughout.

### Findings
- **low** FR-41 "Progressive and authored awards" is a restatement (§4.6) — its content is FR-38 (Jackpot seeding) and FR-21 (achievement menu); the only new consequence is "persist per player for the game; both reset at game end." As a separate FR it will become a separate story with nothing to build. *Fix:* fold its consequence into FR-38 and FR-21 and delete FR-41, or retitle it as the per-player persistence rule for all Mode state.
- **low** FR-31 "Lawlor's test" is a design ethic, not a feature (§4.5) — nothing can be implemented against it; its consequence ("verified per shot in the feel test") is an acceptance criterion for FR-26–FR-30. Keeping it is defensible, but a story writer will not know what to do with it. *Fix:* keep the ID but mark it "acceptance rule for §4.5, not a standalone deliverable," or move it into the §4.5 description and cite it from each shot FR.

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis is stated in §0 and §1 and it constrains the rest: §4.2's "This is where the project's schedule will actually go" is prioritisation by thesis, not by ease. SM-1 through SM-5 validate the thesis rather than measuring activity — there is no DAU-shaped metric anywhere, which is correct for a table nobody is required to play. Counter-metrics are present and each names what it counterbalances (SM-C1 guards §5, SM-C4 guards against "Not tuned forever"). MVP scope kind is *experience*, and §6.1/§6.2 read that way: the wizard mode and 4-ball finale are deferred as escalations of the experience, not as features cut for time.

### Findings
- **low** SM-2 under-cites the moment (§7) — "Validates FR-36–FR-38" names the rules, but the dragon-fire moment as described in UJ-1 and §1 is also made of FR-29 (Mouth fires "toward the flippers"), FR-45 ("the War start is the brightest event"), and FR-48 ("the War start cue is distinct"). If SM-2 fails, those are where the fix lives. *Fix:* add FR-29, FR-45, FR-48 to SM-2's validation list.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

Most FRs carry a testable consequence, and several are exemplary: FR-7 ("identical inputs produce identical outcomes at 30, 60, and 120 Hz"), FR-8 (replay), FR-9 ("setting the control to zero produces no hops"), FR-15 ("score is never zeroed by Tilt"), FR-19 ("a drain 1 s after the timer reads zero is still saved"), FR-44 ("no state uses a colour outside the mapping"), FR-47 ("replacing a sound file changes the sound and nothing else"). Feel FRs (FR-5, FR-6) honestly delegate to the feel test, which is the right answer for a hobby project with a Reference machine.

Where it falls short is §4.6. The Mode FRs are individually clear but their interactions — the thing the Rules layer actually is — are unspecified, and the Lock lane is overloaded (mode start, ball lock, War start, strike, Jackpot collect) without a precedence rule. A story writer can build each FR and still not know what the machine does when two of them apply to the same ball. Separately, scoring depth is uneven: FR-33/35/38 carry starting values, FR-18/26/28/34 do not.

### Findings
- **high** War ⇄ Quick multiball ⇄ Lock ball counts are unspecified (FR-34, FR-36, FR-37, FR-39) — FR-34 says Quick multiball "cannot start during the War," but nothing says the War cannot start during Quick multiball: two locked + two in play + the FR-37 eject = four balls, contradicting "three balls in play" and the addendum's "4-ball reserved for a later finale." Likewise FR-36 during Quick multiball: locking a ball and serving a new one (FR-36) keeps two in play, but locking without a serve ends the Mode by FR-34's "ends when one ball remains." FR-37 disables the Lock only "during the War"; addendum §3 says "disabled during multiball" — the broader rule is the right one and it lives in the wrong file. *Fix:* state in FR-36 that the Lock is disabled during any multiball (Lock-lane shot scores as a Dragon hit), and in FR-37 that the War cannot start while Quick multiball is running (the Lock-lane shot is held/queued or scores as a hit). Add to FR-40 or a new consequence.
- **medium** Jackpot collect location contradicts itself (FR-38) — the sentence says "collected at the Dragon" and the same FR's assumption says "collected at the Mouth via the Lock lane," while FR-37 says a Lock-lane shot during the War "counts as a Dragon strike." Whether a lane shot with the Jackpot lit is a strike or a collect, and whether the ball actually enters the (disabled) Lock device and is Mouth-ejected, is undecidable from the text. Also unstated: whether the War continues after the Jackpot is collected (FR-39 says it ends "when one ball remains") and whether the Jackpot relights. *Fix:* pick one — e.g. "Jackpot lit: any Dragon hit (body or lane) collects; the War continues until one ball remains; Jackpot does not relight within the same War."
- **medium** Bonus multiplier has no advancement rule (FR-20) — "multiplied by a bonus multiplier" appears once; no FR says what raises it (inlane/outlane completion via FR-25 is the convention, but it is not written). *Fix:* add a consequence to FR-20 or FR-25: "completing the inlane/outlane set advances the bonus multiplier (×2 … ×N `[ASSUMPTION]`); multiplier resets each ball."
- **medium** Mode-lit ∧ Lock-lit precedence at the Lock lane (FR-32, FR-36) — the §4.6 assumption says the lane is the mode-start scoop "when the Lock is not lit," which implies Lock wins. If so, a lit Hurry-up is stranded until two balls are locked and the War is played, which may be intended but is nowhere stated; and FR-32's flipper-select "before the Mouth ejects" has no equivalent for the locked case. *Fix:* one consequence in FR-32: "if the Lock is lit, a Lock-lane shot locks; lit Modes remain lit and start on the next Lock-lane shot after the Lock is full or unlit."
- **medium** Tilt during multiball is single-ball (FR-15) — "flippers go dead, the ball drains" says nothing about the other balls in play, whether the active Mode ends, or what happens to balls in the Lock (the addendum §4 says "flippers and autofire coils disabled until drain," which the PRD does not carry). *Fix:* "all balls in play drain; active Modes end; locked balls stay locked; the Mouth does not fire."
- **low** Placeholder shots and missing award values (FR-18, FR-21, FR-26, FR-28, FR-33, FR-34) — "collected at a designated shot" (FR-21, FR-33) names no shot; Skill shot "fixed value," Spinner "per rotation," DRAGON "awards," and Quick multiball "awards on Dragon hits and Ramp shots" have no values while FR-33/35/38 do. Stories either invent or block. *Fix:* name the shot (the Insert colour grammar in FR-44 already implies the collect shot has a red Insert — say which one) and add `[ASSUMPTION]` starting values or "tunable, default X" for each.
- **low** Unbounded phrases — FR-11 "any legal ball speed" (define via the physics cap), FR-14 "Repeated or hard Nudges" and "the debounce window" (no threshold or duration), FR-23 "for a period" (no seconds), FR-19 "shows a hurry-up state before expiry" (no lead time), FR-54 "Firefox is best-effort" (no definition of done). Each is fine as a tunable but needs a default. *Fix:* add a default value with `[ASSUMPTION]` to each.
- **low** NFR-1 hardware target is a category, not a device — "mid-range 2022+ laptop GPU" cannot be measured; UJ-1 says the author plays on a Mac. *Fix:* name the author's machine as the reference device and one Windows equivalent.

## Scope honesty — strong

§5 Non-Goals does real work ("Not an engine … the boundary most likely to erode"; "Not tuned forever"). §6.2 lists eight deferrals with the reason for each. Eighteen `[ASSUMPTION]` tags sit exactly where the author inferred rather than confirmed — including two NFR targets that admit "the research quantifies none" — and every one is indexed. Unverified numbers are labelled unverified (FR-22, addendum §2 "Match 8% is unverified"). Open-items density (7 OQs + 18 assumptions + 1 NOTE) is high in absolute terms but nearly all are tuning values or geometry, which is appropriate for a hobby PRD that precedes the drawn playfield.

### Findings
- **low** WebGPU "enhancement" has no defined content (FR-54, §6.1) — WebGL2 is "fully playable, not degraded in feel," so what WebGPU adds is unstated; a story writer could silently spawn a WebGPU epic. *Fix:* either state what it enhances (e.g. lightmap resolution, post-effects) or mark it `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` with the Babylon.js progressive path kept as an architecture option.

## Downstream usability — strong

The Glossary is 45 terms, used consistently across FRs, UJs and SMs (spot-checked Backglass, Lock/Mouth, Mode, Hot seat, Ball save, Reference machine, War). FR-1…FR-55 are contiguous with no gaps or duplicates; UJ-1…4, SM-1…5, SM-C1…4, NFR-1…9 likewise. Every cross-reference resolves (SM-3 → FR-1/3/24/49/53/54; SM-4 → FR-5/6/29/31; NFR-2 → FR-7; §5 → UJ-4). The Assumptions Index round-trips completely — all 18 inline tags are indexed and every index entry has an inline tag. Each §4 group opens with a description that carries its own context, so sections can be pulled out alone. The addendum §8 list of qualitative intents is a genuinely useful thing to hand to epic creation.

### Findings
- **medium** The addendum disclaims what it actually specifies (addendum §0 "Nothing here is a requirement; the PRD is") — several behaviours exist only there and are more specific than the FR they elaborate: Lock disabled during any multiball (§3, vs FR-37 War only); Ball search as a three-phase protocol with a defined failure action (§4, vs FR-23 "escalating"); tilt disables autofire coils (§4, vs FR-15); per-mode Ball save overriding global (§4, vs FR-19); layered adjustments store (§4, no FR). A story writer who honours the disclaimer and reads only `prd.md` will miss them. *Fix:* either lift each delta into the relevant FR's consequences, or change the disclaimer to "§3–§4 are normative detail for FR-15, FR-19, FR-23, FR-36–FR-37."
- **low** Glossary gaps — "Attract" (FR-1, FR-24, FR-49, FR-51, UJ-2), "Strike" (UJ-1, FR-28, FR-37, FR-38), and "Bonus multiplier" (FR-20) are each load-bearing and undefined. *Fix:* add three entries.

## Shape fit — strong

Hobby/solo, chain-top into architecture and stories, experience product with a walk-up and a headline moment. Four UJs with a protagonist each is the right density — not over-formalised, and the UJs are used (UJ-1 and UJ-4 are cited from §4 descriptions and SMs). The FR-plus-Consequences form is well matched to story creation. The PRD does not name `bmad-ux` as a consumer even though Walk-up, Backglass and Colour grammar have real UX content; for a solo author who will design those in the build that is a defensible omission, not a shape error. Length (~55 FRs, ~390 lines plus addendum) is proportionate to a one-table game.
