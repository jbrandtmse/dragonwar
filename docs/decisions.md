They did land together (2026-09-03): one ball now closes `s_loop_r_in`, `s_loop_r_out`, `s_loop_l_out` and `s_loop_l_in` and then `s_inlane_l`, and the mirror for the Left Loop, across a swept entry band -- and the plunge still crosses the top (max y 1053.19 mm) and reaches the left bat. |
