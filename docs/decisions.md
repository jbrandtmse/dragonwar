| Spinner model (OQ-6 geometry; AD-6, PRD:71, FR-26) | A **pass-through gate, not a collision body**: a ball crossing `sw_spinner`'s zone imparts rotation proportional to entry speed, and the spinner closes `s_spinner` **once per revolution until it decays** (2026-09-03, author) -- **AD-6 AMENDED 2026-09-03** | A rigid `col_` body the ball strikes (13+ measured variants across both Loop paths, all reverted); closing the review finding as an accepted defect (`high_waived`) rather than `by-design` | Two of the project's own artifacts disagreed: `prd.md:71` puts the Spinner on one of the two Loops, while AD-6's Rule required it to spin "from ball contact". On the delivered orbit geometry both cannot hold. Story 2.1c measured thirteen-plus rigid-body variants across BOTH candidate ball paths -- the ascending 9 mm column and the orbit's own descending return, which *are* the orbit's paths -- and every variant that genuinely contacted the ball produced a permanent DW-119-class stall, because a static rigid body cannot graze a 13.495 mm-radius ball with the precision those paths allow. Decided on the physical argument that a real spinner is a freely-rotating **gate the ball passes through**, not an obstacle it strikes -- so the analytic swept-segment zone test (AD-11) is the *correct* model, and the thirteen failures were failing for the right reason. "Once per revolution until it decays" is deliberately preserved because **FR-26 awards per rotation**; dropping it would trade one artifact conflict for another. **Story 2.3 owns the spin and decay mechanism**, driven off the zone crossing. Because a node nothing collides with is not `col_` under AD-11's prefix contract, `col_spinner_l` is renamed `vis_spinner_l` in the device-behaviour story, batched with `bd_lock`'s golden re-record (either change alone moves `assetHash` and breaks all five goldens, so batching costs one re-record, not two). The 2.1c finding is closed **`by-design`, not `high_waived`** -- with AD-6 amended the node is *intentionally* non-colliding, so the premise is corrected rather than a defect accepted. |
